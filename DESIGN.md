# Design system: Measured Impact

## Direction

The public site follows the supplied reference's useful structure: a clean white navigation bar, one focused centered statement, restrained blue accents, and a large product preview rising from a quiet blue-gray field. The visual language is rewritten for a serious developer tool rather than copied from the reference.

The product route becomes denser without becoming decorative. It uses white technical surfaces, fine rules, compact monospaced labels, and small red and green evidence states. The result should look like software an engineering team could use, not a themed dashboard.

## Typography

- Source Sans 3 is the only primary family. It is sober, legible, and purpose-built for interface and editorial work.
- Display type uses tight tracking and plain weight rather than a decorative face.
- Cascadia Mono is reserved for paths, evidence labels, score ranges, and operational data.
- Body copy stays at 16px or larger on marketing and method pages; compact analyzer annotations remain at least 11px with strong contrast.

## Color and shape

- White is the default surface and pale blue-gray is the only large-area wash.
- Measured blue identifies navigation, actions, graph direction, and structural emphasis.
- Red is reserved for review risk and missing evidence.
- Green is reserved for confirmed test evidence and complete status.
- Large rounded corners appear only on the hero product frame. Operational controls use 6–8px corners.
- No gradients, glass panels, neon, or ornamental illustration systems.

## Layout rules

- Every route has one descriptive h1.
- The home hero is spacious; the analyzer is intentionally dense.
- Cards are used only for discrete records or product-preview nodes, never as default section wrappers.
- Marketing sections use borders and whitespace to establish hierarchy.
- Mobile layouts stack without horizontal page overflow, and controls remain keyboard reachable.

## Product truth

- The landing-page preview uses the same concepts and sample values as the working analyzer.
- The score is always called review attention, never defect probability.
- Unknowns remain visible and are never softened into confident copy.
- A local model may summarize evidence but cannot change graph edges or score factors.
